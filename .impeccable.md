## Design Context

### Users
QA engineers and testers who work with Jira tickets, test cases, and Playwright evidence. They use the chat while diagnosing tickets, generating cases, and reviewing run results — often mid-flow, needing clarity over decoration.

### Brand Personality
Calm, capable, precise. Professional without being corporate; warm amber against deep teal — confidence and focus, not urgency or playfulness.

### Aesthetic Direction
Dark teal surfaces, amber accent, Instrument Sans + Newsreader. Editorial-technical: readable prose first, soft elevation, restrained motion. Anti-references: purple AI gradients, neon glow, cream+terracotta, dense dashboard chrome, pill-cluster clutter.

### Design Principles
1. Prose and evidence are the product — messages should read like a clear work conversation, not a card stack.
2. User vs agent should be obvious at a glance without heavy chrome.
3. Tool activity is secondary context; never compete with the answer.
4. Preserve existing tokens and type; refine rhythm, hierarchy, and affordances.
5. Respect reduced motion; keep touch targets and contrast usable.
